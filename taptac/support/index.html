TapTac Support

Need help with installation, gameplay, online matches, or a nearby-watch match?

Email info@huzstudio.com with your Apple Watch model, watchOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. Please do not include passwords or private tokens.

Before contacting us

  • Ensure both players have a network connection for online play.
  • For nearby matchmaking, keep both apps open in the foreground on compatible Apple Watches.
  • Install the latest available TapTac build from TestFlight or the App Store.
